PinDrop is operated by Orvylix Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the PinDrop website.

Temporary files may be created during the download process and may be automatically removed after a limited period.

Children

PinDrop is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
